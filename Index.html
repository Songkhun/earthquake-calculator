Earthquake Calculator

Choose an earthquake → the app downloads real seismograms → picks P and S → locates the epicentre → computes the magnitude. You check the picks and correct them.

Find another earthquake in the USGS catalogue

2Seismograms and automatic picks

Real broadband records from EarthScope (IRIS), converted to ground displacement in µm (0.05–2 Hz). Black = vertical (Z), blue = radial (R). The app picked P and S automatically. If a pick looks wrong, drag its line; everything below updates. Untick a station to leave it out.

3Distance from S − P

Each S − P time gives the distance to the earthquake, read from the iasp91 travel-time table at the catalogue depth (TravelTimes sheet).

4Epicentre

A circle of radius Δ around each station. The app finds the point that best fits all the circles (★). The grey ◆ is the catalogue epicentre, for comparison.

5Magnitude

Formulas from the lecture. mb from the largest P-wave swing on Z (before S, at most 20 s), Ms from the largest swing after S on R. Δ from your epicentre. Both formulas are defined for 20° < Δ < 160°; closer stations give rough values, as in the worksheet. Red = more than 1 unit from the median (check the picks or the instrument).
